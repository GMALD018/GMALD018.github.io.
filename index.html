Gerardo Maldonado

Sociology Student 📚

I am Gerardo Maldonado, a recent high school graduate with an interest in sociology and the ways people, communities, and institutions shape everyday life. I am curious about how culture, education, social expectations, and public policy influence the opportunities people have. Sociology appeals to me because it offers ways to look beyond individual experiences and understand the larger social patterns connected to them. I hope to keep developing the knowledge and skills that will help me explore these questions thoughtfully.

I graduated from California Virtual Academies in 2026 with a 4.0 GPA. Completing high school through a virtual learning environment required me to take responsibility for organizing my time, keeping track of assignments, and staying focused on long-term academic goals. I also completed two college courses through dual enrollment at Norco College. That experience introduced me to college-level coursework while I was still in high school and helped me become more familiar with the expectations of higher education. I value the opportunity to continue learning and to build on the academic foundation I have developed.

My academic interests include social behavior, culture, inequality, education, government, public policy, and community life. I am especially interested in how social environments affect people's choices and experiences, and how institutions can influence access to resources and opportunities. As I continue my education, I want to strengthen my ability to read carefully, evaluate information, communicate clearly, and think critically about complex issues. These skills are important in sociology because understanding a social issue requires considering different perspectives and examining evidence rather than relying only on assumptions.

I also value learning outside the classroom. Brazilian jiu-jitsu has been an important part of my personal development. Progressing through training and earning a blue belt has required consistency, patience, discipline, and a willingness to learn from mistakes. Training has reminded me that improvement usually comes from steady effort over time. It has also encouraged me to stay composed when facing challenges and to treat feedback as an opportunity to improve.

My goal is to continue exploring sociology and discover how I can apply what I learn in meaningful ways. I am interested in opportunities that allow me to learn about people and communities, develop research and communication skills, and contribute positively to the environment around me. I believe a strong education involves more than earning grades; it also means becoming a thoughtful, responsible person who can listen to others, consider different viewpoints, and approach problems with curiosity. I look forward to building on my education, learning from new experiences, and finding ways to connect my interests with future academic and professional opportunities.
